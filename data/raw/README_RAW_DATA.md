# Raw vendor files (replication archive only)

These are the exact Yahoo Finance exports (via yfinance, auto_adjust=False) used for v3 and v4, obtained from
github.com/hjyssg/tqqq_simulation. Their SHA-256 hashes are in data/v4_results/data_manifest.csv. They are
included so reviewers can reproduce every table. `.gitignore` keeps them out of the public repository
because Yahoo's terms restrict redistribution; do not publish them.
